HOW TO PLAY

    
Pick a friend and fight. Empty the other fighter's health bar to win the round. First to win 2 rounds wins the match. Every fighter has one special move per round. Tournament: knockout for 4, 8 or 16 fighters. Whoever joins plays; the empty seats are CPU fighters. Join with a code or an invite. Practice: try your combos and special on a training dummy that stands still and can't be knocked out with endless health (no timer, your special comes back each time); a panel lists each hit of your combo with its damage and what it did, and the total.

Taller fighters reach further with their punches and kicks; shorter fighters move faster (walking and dashing) by the same proportion, so it's even.

    
Any 4 hits of Punch and Kick make a combo; the 4th hit is a finisher picked by the buttons you pressed. Ending with a Kick launches them into the air. Each hit must land for the combo to continue. Later hits do a bit less damage.

    
Special moves

    

      
FighterSpecialDamageEffect

      
    

    
Once per round. Specials can't be blocked, dodged or rolled out of, and hitting someone doesn't cancel their special: they can't be hurt until it has landed. If you both press special, whoever pressed first goes first, and the other one's special comes right after its whole animation ends.
